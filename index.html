Paper Aces

Two pilots · one sky · turn to the page

How a turn works

Each pilot sees only their own page: the view from the cockpit, with the enemy wherever your last move left him. Both of you choose a maneuver in secret. When the choices are in, both aircraft move at once and you turn to a new page.

Line the enemy up in your ring sight, close enough and at the same height, and your guns hit. Eight hits and he goes down. Read his wings: a tilted aircraft is turning, a tail means he is running, and a full silhouette coming at you means he is about to fire too.

Rules of the air

  1. Hits land when the enemy sits within about 15° of your nose, under 180 m away, and within one altitude band.
  2. Point-blank (under 70 m, inside 22°) is a double hit.
  3. Some maneuvers lock out others next turn: no tight turn straight out of a dive, no Immelmann from a slow crawl, and so on. Locked cards are struck through.
  4. If the aircraft drift more than 400 m apart, both pilots wheel around and re-engage head-on.
  5. Pass too close and you both take a hit from the collision.
